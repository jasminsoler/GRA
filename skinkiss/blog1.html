Simple Skincare Routine

A Beginner's Guide to Building a Routine You'll Actually Want to Use

A simple, beginner-friendly guide to cleanser, treatments, moisturizer, sunscreen, product order, skin types, and building a routine that feels intentional—not overwhelming.

THE BEAUTY DESK 01

Skincare can feel a little overwhelming. One minute you're looking for a moisturizer, and suddenly you're comparing serums, exfoliating acids, toners, essences, masks, oils, retinoids—and wondering if your face really needs a 12-step routine.

The good news? It doesn't. Learning how to build a simple skincare routine is less about collecting every product on the shelf and more about understanding what your skin actually needs.

For most beginners, a thoughtful routine can start with just a few basics: cleanser, moisturizer, and sunscreen in the morning, with cleansing and moisturizing again at night.

Once those steps feel comfortable, you can consider adding targeted products based on your individual skin concerns and preferences.

The goal isn't to do the most. It's to create a routine you can actually stick with.

Let's make it simple.

How Do You Build a Simple Skincare Routine?

A simple skincare routine typically starts with three essentials: cleanser, moisturizer, and sunscreen.

In the morning, cleanse, moisturize, and finish with broad-spectrum SPF. At night, cleanse and moisturize.

Once your basic routine is established, you can gradually add targeted products such as serums or treatments based on your skin's needs.

THE FOUNDATION

Why Keep Your Skincare Routine Simple?

A simple routine is easier to understand, easier to maintain, and gives you a better idea of how your skin responds to individual products.

When you introduce several new products at once, it can become difficult to tell which product is helping, irritating, or simply unnecessary.

A simpler approach also gives you room to focus on consistency. Your skincare routine doesn't need to feel like homework.

It can be a few intentional minutes in the morning and evening— a small ritual that helps you take care of your skin and yourself.

Think of it as a foundation. Once you understand the basics, you can build from there.

THE ROUTINE EDIT

Four Steps to Build From

01

CLEANSE

Start With Clean Skin

Cleansing removes dirt, excess oil, makeup, sunscreen, and other buildup from the skin's surface.

Choose a cleanser that feels comfortable rather than one that leaves your skin feeling extremely tight or stripped. If your skin feels uncomfortable after cleansing, that's worth paying attention to.

GOOD TO KNOW

  1. Double cleansing is optional.
  2. For heavier makeup or sunscreen days, some people prefer an oil-based or balm cleanser followed by a water-based cleanser.
  3. You don't need to double cleanse simply because someone online does.
02

TARGETED CARE

Add a Serum or Treatment—If You Need One

A serum is a lightweight skincare product formulated to deliver specific ingredients to the skin. Different serums can target different concerns, such as hydration, uneven tone, or the appearance of fine lines.

But serum is not automatically a required step. If you're just starting out, you can absolutely build your routine without one.

INGREDIENTS YOU MAY ENCOUNTER

  1. Hyaluronic acid — commonly used in hydrating formulas.
  2. Niacinamide — commonly used in formulas for uneven tone, oiliness, and overall texture.
  3. Vitamin C — an antioxidant commonly used in products designed to support brighter, more even-looking skin.
  4. Retinoids — vitamin A derivatives commonly used in products targeting signs of skin aging and other concerns.
More isn't always better.
03

HYDRATION

Moisturize

Moisturizer helps support the skin's moisture barrier and reduce water loss from the skin. And yes—this step is useful even if your skin tends to be oily.

If you have dry skin, you may prefer a richer cream. If your skin is oily or combination, you may prefer a lightweight lotion or gel. If your skin is sensitive, keeping your routine simple may be helpful.

Your moisturizer doesn't need to be the most expensive product on your shelf. It just needs to work comfortably for you.

04

DAYTIME ESSENTIAL

Finish Your Morning Routine With Sunscreen

Sunscreen is the final step of your morning skincare routine. Look for a broad-spectrum sunscreen and follow the product's directions for application and reapplication.

Sunscreen is especially important when you're trying to maintain an even-looking complexion because UV exposure can contribute to premature skin aging and can worsen the appearance of some types of discoloration.

Think of it as the final layer of your morning skincare ritual— not an optional accessory.

ROUTINE ORDER

What Order Should You Apply Skincare Products?

A good general rule: thinner textures first, thicker textures later.

Follow any specific instructions provided by the product manufacturer, especially when you're using targeted treatments.

Morning: Cleanser → Serum/Treatment → Moisturizer → Sunscreen

Night: Cleanser → Serum/Treatment → Moisturizer

If you're using multiple treatments, don't assume that every active ingredient belongs together. Some combinations can increase the chance of irritation. When in doubt, keep things simple and introduce products gradually.

KNOW YOUR SKIN

How Do You Build a Routine for Your Skin Type?

Your skin type can give you a starting point when choosing products, but it doesn't have to dictate every decision.

Dry skin: Look for gentle cleansing and moisturizing products that help your skin feel comfortable and hydrated.

Oily skin: Lightweight, comfortable formulas may be easier to incorporate. Avoid assuming that oily skin should be aggressively stripped.

Combination skin: Different areas of your face may have different needs.

Sensitive skin: A minimalist routine can be especially helpful when you're identifying what your skin tolerates comfortably.

Normal skin: You still don't need a complicated routine. Focus on the basics and add targeted products only when they serve a purpose.

Skin type and skin concerns are not the same thing. Skin is nuanced.

Questions We Get Asked

A few beginner skincare questions worth keeping bookmarked.

01 What is the simplest skincare routine for beginners?

The simplest routine is usually a cleanser, moisturizer, and sunscreen. In the morning, cleanse, moisturize, and finish with broad-spectrum sunscreen. At night, cleanse and moisturize.

02 What order should I apply my skincare products?

A simple general order is cleanser, serum or treatment, moisturizer, and sunscreen in the morning. At night, use cleanser, serum or treatment, and moisturizer. Follow individual product directions.

03 Do I need a serum in my skincare routine?

No. A serum is not required for a basic routine. It can be useful when you have a specific skincare goal, but beginners can establish a comfortable cleanser, moisturizer, and sunscreen routine first.

04 How many skincare products should I use?

There is no ideal number for everyone. A simple routine can work well with three core products. Additional products should have a purpose rather than being added simply to make the routine longer.

05 Should I use moisturizer if I have oily skin?

Yes. Oily skin can still benefit from moisturizer. Look for a formula that feels comfortable and suits your preferences; lightweight lotions or gel-style moisturizers may feel more comfortable for some people.

06 How long does it take for a skincare routine to work?

The timeline varies depending on the product, ingredients, concern, and individual skin. Focus on using products consistently and evaluating how your skin responds over time rather than expecting overnight results.

07 Can I start a skincare routine if I don't know my skin type?

Absolutely. Begin with gentle, basic products and pay attention to how your skin feels and responds. As you learn more about your skin, you can adjust your routine and product choices.
