7 Habits That Could Be Holding Your Glow Back

Before you buy another product, look at the routine you already have. These common habits can make skincare more complicated than it needs to be.

Your skincare shelf is full. You've found your cleanser. You have a moisturizer you actually like. Maybe you've added a serum, a mask, an exfoliant, and a couple of cute little tools.

So why does your skin still feel like it's not quite giving what you want?

Before you buy another product, take a look at your routine. Sometimes the issue isn't that your skin needs more. A few everyday habits—like over-exfoliating, skipping sunscreen, or constantly changing products—can make a routine more complicated than it needs to be.

The good news? Most skincare mistakes are fixable. Here are seven common habits to rethink if your routine isn't feeling quite right.

What Are the Most Common Skincare Mistakes?

Some of the most common skincare mistakes include over-exfoliating, using too many products at once, skipping sunscreen, cleansing too aggressively, picking at your skin, introducing products too quickly, and neglecting your skin's moisture barrier.

A simpler, more consistent routine can make it easier to understand what your skin needs and how it responds.

02

MISTAKE NO. 01

Are You Over-Exfoliating Your Skin?

Yes, exfoliating too frequently or aggressively can irritate the skin. Exfoliation is designed to help remove dead skin cells from the skin's surface. Depending on the product, this can involve physical exfoliation or chemical exfoliating ingredients.

But more exfoliation doesn't automatically mean smoother-looking skin. If you're exfoliating frequently while also using other potentially irritating active ingredients, your routine may simply be doing too much.

What Does Over-Exfoliation Look Like?

  • Increased dryness
  • Persistent redness
  • Stinging or burning
  • Tightness
  • Increased sensitivity
  • Skin that feels uncomfortable after applying products

The Simple Fix

Take a step back. Reduce unnecessary exfoliation and focus on gentle cleansing, moisturizing, and sun protection while your skin settles.

TOO MANY PRODUCTS

Are You Using Too Many Skincare Products?

A longer routine isn't automatically a better routine. Skincare can be fun, but when you're layering multiple serums, acids, treatments, masks, oils, and tools without knowing how they work together, it becomes difficult to understand what's actually benefiting your skin.

It can also make irritation more likely, particularly when several active ingredients are involved.

Try a skincare reset: Cleanser → Moisturizer → Sunscreen. Then gradually add other products when you have a specific reason to use them.

03
DAYTIME ESSENTIAL

Are You Skipping Sunscreen?

Skipping sunscreen can leave your skin more exposed to UV radiation, which contributes to premature skin aging and can worsen some forms of discoloration.

Look for a broad-spectrum sunscreen and use it according to the product's directions. Broad-spectrum sunscreen is designed to provide protection against both UVA and UVB radiation.

Your morning routine can be simple: Cleanse → Treat → Moisturize → SPF.

“Sometimes the best skincare move is actually simplifying.”

MISTAKE NO. 04

Are You Cleansing Too Aggressively?

Your cleanser doesn't need to leave your skin feeling “squeaky clean.” That ultra-tight feeling after washing your face might seem like proof that you've removed everything, but excessive cleansing or harsh scrubbing can leave your skin feeling dry and uncomfortable.

How Should You Cleanse?

  1. Wet your face with lukewarm water.
  2. Apply your cleanser.
  3. Massage gently with your fingertips.
  4. Rinse thoroughly.
  5. Pat your skin dry rather than aggressively rubbing it.

If you're using a cleansing tool, the same rule applies: gentle is enough.

Your face isn't a dish that needs scrubbing.

MISTAKE NO. 05

Are You Picking at Your Skin?

Picking, squeezing, or constantly touching blemishes can irritate the skin and may increase the chance of lingering marks or scarring.

We know. That tiny little spot is practically begging to be squeezed. But your fingers don't need to be involved. Repeatedly touching your face can also transfer oils, dirt, and other substances from your hands to your skin.

Try This Instead

When you notice yourself reaching for a blemish: step away from the mirror. Apply your normal skincare products, leave the area alone, and give your routine time.

If you're concerned about persistent or severe breakouts, a dermatologist can help you determine appropriate treatment options.

05

MISTAKE NO. 06

Are You Changing Your Routine Too Often?

Constantly changing products can make it difficult to understand how your skin responds to your routine.

One week it's a new serum. The next week it's a trending toner. Then someone recommends an exfoliant. Suddenly your bathroom counter looks like a beauty store—and you have no idea what's actually working.

Give Your Routine Some Breathing Room

Whenever possible, introduce new products one at a time. This gives you a clearer picture of how your skin responds and makes your routine easier to maintain.

Skincare is not a race to collect every ingredient. It's about finding a combination that fits your skin and your lifestyle.

MISTAKE NO. 07

Are You Forgetting About Your Skin Barrier?

Your skin barrier is the outermost layer of your skin that helps protect it and regulate moisture. When people talk about a “healthy skin barrier,” they're generally referring to skin that is functioning normally and feels comfortable rather than persistently dry, irritated, or sensitive.

Your routine can support your skin barrier by avoiding unnecessary irritation and maintaining hydration.

What Can a Barrier-Friendly Routine Look Like?

  • Use a gentle cleanser.
  • Moisturize regularly.
  • Avoid excessive exfoliation.
  • Introduce active ingredients gradually.
  • Protect your skin from UV exposure.
  • Pay attention when your skin feels irritated.

Sometimes the best thing you can do for your routine is stop adding things.

THE BONUS

Could Your Skincare Tools Be Part of the Problem?

Skincare tools can be a beautiful addition to your routine—but they still need to be used thoughtfully.

Use Gentle Pressure

You don't need to press hard.

Keep Your Tools Clean

Follow the manufacturer's cleaning instructions and allow tools to dry properly.

Don't Use Tools on Irritated Skin

If your skin is already uncomfortable, skip anything that adds friction or pressure.

Don't Use Tools Simply Because They're Trending

Ask yourself: What is this tool designed to do? Does it fit my routine? Do I actually enjoy using it? If the answer is yes, keep it. If not, you don't need it.

Questions We Get Asked

What are the most common skincare mistakes?

Common mistakes include over-exfoliating, using too many products at once, skipping sunscreen, cleansing too aggressively, picking at your skin, introducing products too quickly, and neglecting your skin's moisture barrier.

How do I know if I'm over-exfoliating?

Possible signs include increased dryness, persistent redness, stinging or burning, tightness, increased sensitivity, or discomfort after applying products. These signs can have many possible causes, so they don't automatically mean you've damaged your skin.

Is a longer skincare routine better?

No. A longer routine isn't automatically a better routine. A simple foundation can make it easier to understand what your skin needs and how it responds.

Why is sunscreen important in a skincare routine?

Sunscreen helps protect skin from UV radiation. Look for a broad-spectrum sunscreen and use it according to the product's directions.

How should I cleanse my face?

Use lukewarm water, apply your cleanser, massage gently with your fingertips, rinse thoroughly, and pat your skin dry rather than aggressively rubbing it.

How often should I change my skincare routine?

Constantly changing products can make it difficult to understand how your skin responds. Whenever possible, introduce new products one at a time so you can evaluate them more clearly.

What is the skin barrier?

Your skin barrier is the outermost layer of your skin that helps protect it and regulate moisture. A barrier-friendly routine focuses on avoiding unnecessary irritation and maintaining hydration.

When should I reset my skincare routine?

Consider simplifying when your skin feels persistently irritated, you're using many products without knowing why, or you're constantly changing products because you're unhappy with your results. For persistent irritation or significant discomfort, consider speaking with a dermatologist.
