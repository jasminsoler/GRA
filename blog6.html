Everything Shower 101

The Ultimate Shower & Skincare Routine, Step by Step

A practical guide to turning an ordinary shower into a full-body reset—without turning Tuesday night into a 27-step beauty marathon.

There are regular showers. And then there are everything showers—the kind where you wash your hair, take your time with body care, do your skincare, put on fresh pajamas, moisturize from head to toe, and suddenly feel like you have your life together.

An everything shower is essentially a longer, more intentional shower routine that combines several personal-care steps into one reset. But you don't need 25 products or a three-hour beauty routine to do it well.

A good everything shower is really about doing the steps that make you feel clean, refreshed, comfortable, and put together.

And when it comes to your face, more steps are not automatically better. The trick is knowing what to do, when to do it, and what you can skip.

What Is an Everything Shower Routine?

An everything shower routine is an extended self-care shower that combines hair care, body cleansing, optional exfoliation or shaving, and other personal-care steps into one intentional ritual.

After the shower, you can finish with body moisturizer and a simple facial skincare routine. The best everything shower is personalized—not necessarily the longest or most complicated one.

THE RESET

What Is an “Everything Shower”?

An everything shower is essentially a full-body reset. Instead of jumping in, washing quickly, and rushing out, you intentionally move through several grooming and self-care steps.

Depending on your preferences, that might include shampoo and conditioner, scalp care, body cleansing, exfoliation, shaving, foot care, body moisturizer, facial skincare, hair care, skincare tools, and a little extra time to relax.

Some people do an everything shower once a week. Others simply do one whenever they have the time and want a reset.

There is no official checklist. And honestly? That's part of the appeal.

SET THE SCENE

Start With the Mood Before You Even Get In

This part is completely optional—but arguably half the fun. An everything shower can be your little weekly reset, so make the environment feel intentional.

Play a favorite playlist, turn on a warm bathroom light, put your phone away, set out your favorite towel, prepare your skincare products beforehand, and have a robe or comfortable pajamas ready.

You don't need a perfectly curated bathroom. The goal is simply to make the experience feel less rushed.

03

HAIR + BODY

Wash Your Hair, Then Cleanse Your Body

If you're washing your hair, it generally makes sense to do this before your facial skincare and body-care finishing steps. Start with shampoo and work it into the scalp according to the product's directions, then condition the lengths and ends as appropriate for your hair type and product.

If your hair-care routine calls for extras, your everything shower can be the day for a hair mask, scalp care, detangling, or a pre-wash treatment. Just remember that not every shower needs every treatment.

Once your hair care is underway, move into body cleansing. Use a body cleanser that feels comfortable on your skin and wash thoroughly, paying attention to areas where sweat, oil, and everyday buildup tend to collect.

An everything shower is partly about the ritual, so slow down instead of rushing through the shower.

04

OPTIONAL EXTRAS

Exfoliate or Shave—But Don't Overdo It

Body exfoliation can be a nice addition to an everything shower, but it is optional. Exfoliation means removing dead skin cells from the surface of the skin. You may see physical exfoliants such as scrubs, as well as chemical exfoliants designed to loosen dead skin cells.

If you use a body scrub, be gentle. You do not need to aggressively scrub your skin to make it feel smooth. If your skin is already irritated, freshly shaved, or uncomfortable, skipping exfoliation may be the better choice.

Shaving is also completely optional. If you shave, give yourself enough time to do it carefully, use a shaving product that provides enough slip, and avoid aggressively scrubbing freshly shaved skin.

Body hair removal is a preference, not a skincare requirement.

05

SKINCARE

Give Your Face Some Attention

Your face does not need an entirely separate 12-step routine just because you're having an everything shower. In fact, this is a great opportunity to keep things simple.

Start with a gentle facial cleanser. If you've been wearing makeup or sunscreen, make sure your cleansing routine is appropriate for removing those products.

Then consider whether your skin actually needs an additional treatment. That could mean a hydrating treatment, a face mask, a targeted skincare product, a gentle facial massage—or nothing extra at all.

Sometimes cleanser + moisturizer is perfectly fine.

06

THE FUN PART

Add a Face Mask If It Fits Your Routine

A face mask can turn an ordinary shower day into a mini spa ritual. Choose the mask based on what your skin needs.

Feeling dry? Consider a hydrating formula. Want oil-absorbing care? A clay-based mask may be an option. Want gentle exfoliation? An exfoliating mask may fit—but consider the other active ingredients already in your routine.

The key is not stacking every treatment you own simply because you have extra time. Your skin does not know that you are having an everything shower.

THE BIGGER PICTURE

The Everything Shower Is About More Than Skincare

An everything shower isn't really about having the most elaborate beauty routine. It's about creating a pause.

It is taking the ordinary things you already do—washing your hair, cleansing your skin, moisturizing—and making them feel intentional.

You don't need expensive products. You don't need a perfect bathroom. You don't need to follow someone else's 27-step checklist.

You simply need a little time and a routine that makes sense for you.

Questions We Get Asked

What is an everything shower?

An everything shower is an extended shower routine where you combine several personal-care steps into one reset. It can include hair care, body care, grooming, and optional skincare treatments.

What order should I do my everything shower routine?

A simple order is hair care → body cleansing → optional exfoliation or shaving → facial cleansing → rinse → body moisturizer → facial skincare → hair finishing steps.

Should I exfoliate during an everything shower?

You can, but exfoliation is optional. Avoid aggressive exfoliation, particularly if your skin is irritated or recently shaved.

Should I wash my face in the shower or after?

Either can work. What matters most is using a facial cleanser appropriate for your skin and routine. Avoid very hot water on your face, especially if your skin tends to feel dry or sensitive.

How often should you have an everything shower?

There is no required frequency. Some people enjoy one once a week, while others do one whenever they have extra time. Your everyday routine can remain much simpler.

What skincare should I do after an everything shower?

Keep it simple. After cleansing, you can use a treatment or serum that fits your routine, followed by moisturizer. If it is daytime, finish with sunscreen.

Can I use a face mask during an everything shower?

Yes, if the specific mask is designed for that type of use. Choose the mask based on your skin's current needs and follow the product's instructions.

Does an everything shower have to take hours?

Absolutely not. A 20–30 minute version can still feel like a complete reset. The goal is intentionality, not duration.
